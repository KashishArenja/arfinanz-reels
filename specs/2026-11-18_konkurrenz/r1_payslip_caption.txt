Most people with a rental flat wait for their tax refund once a year. You don't have to. 🇩🇪

If your flat costs more than the rent brings in (interest, building depreciation/AfA, other costs), the tax office can enter that loss as an allowance (Freibetrag) on your payslip. Then less wage tax is taken every month – not as one refund a year later.

Find your salary 👇 (flat costs €6,600 a year more than the rent)
💶 €50,000 → €1,984 less tax a year = +€165 a month
💶 €60,000 → €2,166 = +€180 a month
💶 €70,000 → €2,349 = +€196 a month
💶 €80,000 → €2,557 = +€213 a month
💶 €90,000 → €2,749 = +€229 a month

How it works:
📝 Apply at your tax office with the form "Antrag auf Lohnsteuer-Ermäßigung" (§39a EStG) – for 2027 you can apply since 1 October
🏠 A newly bought flat counts from the year after you buy it
🧾 With an allowance you have to file a tax return every year

🇮🇳 Back home you showed HR your 80C investments and paid less TDS every month. Germany has the same idea – you just apply at the tax office, not with HR, and there's a real asset behind it. Important: the flat still costs you money each month (repayment), the tax effect is not a reason on its own to buy.

Example calculation, not a guarantee: single, tax class 1, gross salary before tax, 2026 rates (§32a EStG), standard deductions for social insurance, without solidarity surcharge and church tax.
