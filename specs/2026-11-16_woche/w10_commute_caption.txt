Do you drive to work? Save this 📌 – since 2026 your commute is worth more in your tax return.

New from 01.01.2026: €0.38 per km from the FIRST km (before: €0.30 for the first 20 km). 🚗

Example: 30 km one way × 220 work days × €0.38 = €2,508 deduction.
Everyone gets €1,230 automatically (employee allowance) – the €1,278 above that brings money back:
➡️ at 30% tax rate about €383
➡️ at 42% tax rate about €537

Also counts: bus, train, bike – it's per km distance, not per car.

🇮🇳 As an employee in tax class 1 you often don't HAVE to file a tax return – so many expats never do. You can file voluntarily for the last 4 years.
