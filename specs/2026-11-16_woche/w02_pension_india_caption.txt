Planning to go back to India one day? 🇮🇳 Your German state pension doesn't stay behind.

✅ It can be paid to your bank account in India
✅ Any passport – you don't need German citizenship
✅ You need 5 years of contributions. Thanks to the India–Germany social security agreement (2017), your Indian insurance years can help you reach them
💶 The amount is based on your German years only
📄 Once a year you send a life certificate (Lebensbescheinigung)
🔍 Check your pension record (Versicherungsverlauf) at Deutsche Rentenversicherung now – gaps are easier to fix early

🇩🇪 Your German colleague starts paying in at 22, you maybe at 30. Fewer years = smaller pension. That gap is the part worth planning now.
