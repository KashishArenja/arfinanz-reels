Buying a €400,000 flat? The price is not the full bill. On top come the buying costs (Kaufnebenkosten) – and many banks want you to pay them from your own savings, especially as an expat without a long German credit history.

What you pay on top 👇
🏛️ Property transfer tax (Grunderwerbsteuer): 3.5% in Bavaria up to 6.5% in NRW, Brandenburg, Saarland and Schleswig-Holstein
✍️ Notary + land registry: about 2%
🤝 Agent (if there is one): up to 3.57%

For €400,000 that's about €36,000 (Bavaria) to €48,000 (NRW). 🇮🇳 Back home stamp duty works similarly – but in Germany the bank usually won't add it to your loan.
