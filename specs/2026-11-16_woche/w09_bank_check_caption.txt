Blue Card holder and dreaming of your own home? 🏡 This is what German banks look at before they say YES:

🪪 Your residence permit – and how long it's still valid
📈 Your last 3–6 payslips and work contract
✅ Probation finished
🧾 Your SCHUFA record (starts with your first German account)
💶 Savings for the buying costs (about 9–12% of the price)
🇮🇳 Loans and EMIs in India – yes, they count too

Every bank weighs these differently. That's why comparing many banks matters more for expats than for Germans.
