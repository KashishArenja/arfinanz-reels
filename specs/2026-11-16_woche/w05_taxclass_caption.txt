Your wife or husband joined you in Germany? Save this 📌 and check your tax class this week.

Many expats arrive alone and start in tax class 1. When the partner comes later on a family visa and doesn't work yet, nobody changes anything – and you pay too much tax every month.

👉 Married + partner lives in Germany + earns little or nothing:
✅ Joint taxation (Ehegattensplitting) applies
✅ With tax class 3 you get the advantage every month on your payslip
✅ Or at least claim it in your tax return

Example: €72,000 gross salary, partner without income → about €500 more net a month (≈ €6,000 a year).

🇮🇳 In India there's no such thing as tax classes – that's why so many miss this here.
