Renovating your bathroom or kitchen? Save this 📌 before you pay the invoice.

In Germany you get 20% of the labour costs back from your tax – up to €1,200 a year (craftsmen's services, §35a EStG). 🔧

✅ Only labour, travel and machine costs – not the material
✅ The invoice must show labour separately
✅ Pay by bank transfer – cash doesn't count
✅ For the home you live in (owned or rented)
🏠 Renters: check your Nebenkosten bill! Caretaker, stairwell cleaning, garden service, chimney sweep → 20% back too (household services, up to €4,000 a year)
🧾 Claim it in your tax return

Example: €3,000 labour → €600 less tax.

🇮🇳 Many expats never file a tax return in Germany – and leave exactly this money with the tax office.
