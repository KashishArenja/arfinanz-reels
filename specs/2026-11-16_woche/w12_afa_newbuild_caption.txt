Back home you used 80C to save tax. 🇮🇳 In Germany a new rental flat can do something similar – with the 5% declining depreciation (degressive AfA).

Example: €400,000 new-build flat to rent out, building share €340,000
🏗️ New build: 5% in year 1 = €17,000 deduction
🏚️ Older flat: 2% = €6,800 deduction
At a 42% tax rate: ~€7,100 vs ~€2,900 less tax in year 1

Rules (§7 Abs. 5a EStG):
✅ Construction started between 01.10.2023 and 30.09.2029 (or bought in the year it was finished)
✅ You rent it out (not for your own home)
✅ 5% of the remaining value every year – so it goes down slowly

Important: a tax saving is never a reason alone to buy – rent, location and price must fit.
