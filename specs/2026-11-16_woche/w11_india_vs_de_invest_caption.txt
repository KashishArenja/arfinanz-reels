Many of us buy a flat in India as an investment. 🇮🇳 Before you do, compare it with a flat in Germany – where you actually live and earn.

🇮🇳 Flat in India
– Rental yields in big cities are often only about 2–4%
– You manage tenants and repairs from abroad
– No tax benefit in Germany
– Rent in rupees, your life in euros

🇩🇪 Flat in Germany
– Rent is paid in euro
– Building depreciation (2% AfA) and loan interest can lower your German tax
– A German bank can finance a big part of it
– You're nearby to manage it

No answer fits everyone – it depends on your plans (stay or go back?), income and savings.
