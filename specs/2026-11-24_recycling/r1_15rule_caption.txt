The 15% rule decides whether you get your tax money back now – or bit by bit over decades. 🏠

When you buy a flat to rent out in Germany, renovation costs can be treated in two very different ways:

➡️ Stay at or under 15% of the building value (land doesn't count, costs without VAT) in the first 3 years after buying → you can deduct the costs right away from your rental income.
➡️ Go over 15% → the same costs count as "anschaffungsnahe Herstellungskosten" and are only written off together with the building – usually 2% a year, so up to 50 years (§6 Abs. 1 Nr. 1a EStG).

Example: building worth €200,000 → limit €30,000. €30,000 of work = deduct now. €31,000 = all of it spread over the years.

What people get wrong 👇
• It's 15% of the building value, not the purchase price.
• Normal yearly repairs don't count towards the limit.
• The 3 years start on the day the flat becomes yours.
