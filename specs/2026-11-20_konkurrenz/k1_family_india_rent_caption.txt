Family still in India? Save this checklist before your next tax return 📌

You came to Germany first – your wife and kids are still in India? Then your flat here can be a "double household" (doppelte Haushaltsführung, §9 EStG). Most expats never claim it. 💸

🏠 Rent for your flat in Germany: up to €1,000 a month counts (incl. utilities)
✈️ Trips home to your family: max. 1 a week counts
🍛 Food allowance: up to €28 a day for the first 3 months
🇮🇳 Your main home stays in India – your family lives there and you pay for it
📅 It ends when your family moves to Germany
🧾 Claim it in your tax return: Anlage N, section "double household"

Example: €800 rent × 12 = €9,600 + 2 flights home €1,800 + food allowance ~€2,500 = ~€13,900 work costs. At a 30% tax rate that's about €4,200 back.
