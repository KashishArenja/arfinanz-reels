Parents coming to visit you in Germany? 🇮🇳✈️🇩🇪 For the Schengen visa they need travel health insurance with at least €30,000 cover. Many families just buy the cheapest one – and only read the small print in the hospital.

Check these points before you buy:
❌ Old illness (diabetes, heart, blood pressure): often not covered or only in an "acute emergency"
✅ Does the insurer pay the hospital directly – or do you pay first and wait for the refund?
✅ Valid for the whole stay and in all Schengen countries
✅ Transport back to India included
✅ Age limits: from 65 or 70 many plans cost more or cover less
