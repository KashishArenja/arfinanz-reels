You return every bottle for 25 cents Pfand. 🍾 But from 01.01.2027 the state gives you up to €540 a year for your pension – and most people will wait. 🇩🇪

How the new pension depot (Altersvorsorgedepot, AVD) works:
✅ Your first €360 a year: the state adds 50% (max. €180)
✅ €361 to €1,800 a year: the state adds 25% (max. €360)
✅ = €150 a month gets you the full €540
👶 Plus up to €300 per child with Kindergeld

Where most people go wrong:
❌ They wait until mid-2027 – every year without a contract is a bonus lost
❌ They sign the first offer their bank sends
❌ High fees eat the bonus

🇮🇳 No EPF in Germany – nobody opens this for you. You arrived later than your German colleague, so your state pension will be smaller. This bonus helps close part of the gap.
