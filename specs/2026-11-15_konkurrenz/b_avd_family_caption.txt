From 01.01.2027 Germany has a new pension depot (Altersvorsorgedepot, AVD). You pay in – the state adds money every year. Find your family 👇

You pay €150 a month (€1,800 a year):
👤 No kids: +€540 a year from the state
👶 1 child: +€840
👶👶 2 kids: +€1,140
👶👶👶 3 kids: +€1,440
Over 10 years with 2 kids: €11,400 – just from the state, before any returns.

How it's calculated: 50% on your first €360 a year (€180), 25% on the rest up to €1,800 (€360), plus up to €300 per child with Kindergeld (goes to one parent).

🇮🇳 In India your employer fills your EPF automatically. In Germany nobody opens this for you – and as an expat you have fewer German pension years than your colleagues. Start in January: a year you wait is a bonus you never get back.
