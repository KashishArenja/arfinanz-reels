Good salary in Germany? Then 31 December is an important date for your tax. 🇩🇪

What you can pay into a private pension (Basisrente, also called Rürup pension) reduces your taxable income – like 80C back home, but with a much higher limit.

Example 👇
💼 €80,000 a year before tax, single, no church tax
💶 You pay €5,000 into a Basisrente by 31 December
✅ Your tax goes down by about €1,950

Find your salary (same €5,000 paid in):
€60,000 → about €1,650 back
€80,000 → about €1,950 back
€100,000 → about €2,350 back

Good to know:
📅 The money must reach the provider by 31 December to count for 2026
📊 Up to €30,826 a year counts (2026, single) – minus what you and your employer already pay into the German state pension
🔒 Honest: you can't cancel it or take the money out as a lump sum – it is paid as a monthly pension for life, at the earliest from 62

🇮🇳 Moving back to India later? Your Basisrente can still be paid out to you there.
