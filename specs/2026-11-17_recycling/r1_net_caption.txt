Out of €100,000 a year before tax, only about €58,000 reaches your account. Where the rest goes (2026, single, tax class 1):

💶 Income tax + solidarity surcharge: €23,593
💶 Pension: €9,300
💶 Health + long-term care insurance: €7,777
💶 Unemployment insurance: €1,300
✅ You keep: about €58,030 – roughly €4,836 a month

🇮🇳 Back home you had 80C. Germany has no 80C – but your net is not fixed either. Legal ways to keep more: work expenses, a company pension, and a rental flat whose interest, costs and depreciation can lower the tax on your salary. With a Blue Card you can often get the loan for it, too.
