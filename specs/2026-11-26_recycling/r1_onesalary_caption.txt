Example couple in Germany:
💼 Salary 1: €3,600 net – rent, food, life
💼 Salary 2: €2,750 net – €33,000 a year goes into rental flats

How it works:
1️⃣ Clear all small loans first – car loan, credit card, Klarna. Banks check this before anything else.
2️⃣ The saved salary pays the buying costs (about 10% per flat) and a cash buffer.
3️⃣ The bank finances the flat, the tenant pays rent, and the tax office gives part of the costs back (interest + depreciation).
4️⃣ Repeat whenever the savings allow it.

Our example after 20 years: 11 flats, worth about €3.3 million, about €1.7 million loans left = about €1.6 million after loans.

The real secret isn't a huge income – it's discipline and being on the same page as your partner 🤝
