7 things I tell every expat who thinks they can't afford to buy in Germany:

1. Know your budget before you look
Get a financing check first, so you know what a bank will give you. Then book viewings instead of planning for months.

2. The trap: extra costs come from YOUR savings
The bank pays for the flat, not the costs on top: buying tax, notary, land register, agent. Bavaria about 9%, Hamburg about 11%, Berlin about 11.5%. Example Hamburg: €300,000 flat = about €33,000 extra 😳

3. Savings in India count too
Ask your Indian bank early for an English statement – it can take weeks. Banks often count ETFs only partly.

4. Clean up your last 3 months
No overdraft, no Klarna, no random subscriptions. Save a fixed amount right after payday so the bank sees you're in control.

5. Buy the flat that fits, not the dream
Your first flat is your entry ticket. The money it builds pays for the dream flat later.

6. It's not forever
You can always sell it or rent it out – many first homes become the first rental flat.

7. Stop comparing
You don't know who got money from parents or who bought years ago. Only your numbers count.
