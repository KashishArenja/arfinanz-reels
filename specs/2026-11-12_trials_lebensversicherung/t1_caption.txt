If your family lives on your German salary, ask yourself one question: what happens to them if that income stops?

Rule of thumb (Finanztest 12/2026): your term life insurance (in India you'd say "term plan") should pay out about 3–5× your gross yearly salary.
• €50k gross → €150k–€250k
• €70k gross → €210k–€350k
• €100k gross → €300k–€500k

Got a home loan? Add it on top – the loan doesn't disappear with you.

Why expats should check this early: you have fewer years in the German pension system than your German colleague, so the state survivor's pension for your partner can be small.

Next step: write down your gross salary + your open loan amount. That's your starting number.
