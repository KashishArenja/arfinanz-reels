5 signs you should look at term life insurance in Germany:

🏠 You have a home loan – your family would inherit it.
👩‍👧 Your partner earns less or isn't working (yet).
👶 Your kids are growing up here – school, rent, daily life.
🇮🇳 Your parents in India rely on the money you send.
💼 Your salary pays most of the bills.

How much? Rule of thumb (Finanztest 12/2026): about 3–5× your gross yearly salary, plus open loans.
