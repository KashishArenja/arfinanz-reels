In India, a loan is something you kill as fast as possible. In Germany, many smart investors do the opposite – on a RENTED flat. Not because they're careless, but because of 3 simple reasons:

1️⃣ The interest on a loan for a rented flat counts as a cost (Werbungskosten). It lowers the tax on your rental income. Pay the loan off and that tax benefit is gone.

2️⃣ Prices and rents go up over time, your loan amount does not. Example: with 2.5% inflation a year, a €300,000 loan is worth only about €234,000 in today's money after 10 years.

3️⃣ €100,000 extra into the loan saves you about €3,900 interest a year (at ~3.9% for 10 years fixed). The same money can be the start for your next flat.

Important: for your OWN home it's the other way round. There the interest is not tax-deductible – paying it off faster usually makes sense.

Low repayment is not the risk. No plan is the risk: fixed rate, the right to pay extra (Sondertilgung), a cash buffer and a clear plan for the end of the fixed-rate period.
