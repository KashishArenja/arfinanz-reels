Most expats don't struggle because they earn too little – they struggle because nobody showed them how to split it. Here's an example split for one person with €2,500 net a month in Germany:

🏠 Rent + bills ~€1,000 (rent, electricity, internet, TV licence fee)
🛡️ Insurance ~€200 (personal liability, car, disability, contents)
🚨 Emergency money €150 – for the broken washing machine or the flight home
👵 Pension €150
📈 Investing €350
🛒 Food + fun ~€650 (groceries, going out, fuel, streaming)

= €2,500. The €650 for savings is not "what's left at the end" – it goes out first, on payday.

The expat trap: in the first weeks in Germany many sign up for 5–6 insurances at once – some they don't need, some twice (e.g. via employer or credit card). A quick check often shows what you can cancel.
