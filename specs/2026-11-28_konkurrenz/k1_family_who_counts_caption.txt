Money to family in India? 🇮🇳 Germany can give you up to €1,297 tax back per person – but not for everyone. Save this 📌

You send money home every month? The tax office treats it as support payments (Unterhalt, §33a EStG). But only for people you are legally obliged to support:

✅ Mom and Dad – each one counts on its own
✅ Your partner's parents – if you and your partner file a joint tax return
✅ Your wife or husband, if they still live in India
❌ Brother, sister, cousin, uncle – no legal duty, so it doesn't count
⚠️ The person must have (almost) no income or savings of their own
🏦 Pay by bank transfer – cash no longer counts

How much? For India you can deduct up to €3,087 per person a year (2026: €12,348, India counts ¼). At a 42% tax rate that's about €1,297 back per person. Example: both parents, 30% tax rate → about €1,850 a year.

Claim it in your tax return with the form "Anlage Unterhalt" – one form per household you support.
