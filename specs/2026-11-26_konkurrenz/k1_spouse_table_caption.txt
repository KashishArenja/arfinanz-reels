Your husband or wife doesn't work yet and is insured with you for free? From 1 January 2028 that changes for many families in Germany. 🇩🇪

What the new law says (GKV-Beitragssatzstabilisierungsgesetz, passed July 2026):
💶 You pay an extra 2.5% of your salary before tax for your partner
🏢 Your employer pays nothing of it
👶 Your kids stay insured for free
✅ No extra if a child under 12 lives with you, if your partner cares for a relative, has a severe disability or has reached pension age

Find your salary in the table 👇
€3,000 a month → €75 a month, €900 a year
€5,000 a month → €125 a month, €1,500 a year
€6,000 a month → €150 a month, €1,800 a year

🇮🇳 Partner came on a spouse visa, learning German or waiting for a job? That's exactly the household that pays. You have until 2028 to plan – a job above mini-job level, or comparing public vs. private insurance for the whole family.
