The catch: in Germany, a 65-year-old lives on average to 83 (men) or 86 (women) – Destatis, July 2026.

Why this hits expats harder 🇮🇳➡️🇩🇪: if you moved to Germany at 30 or later, you have fewer years in the German state pension. Smaller state pension = you take more from your savings = they run out sooner.

✅ The fix: turn part of your savings into a lifelong pension (private pension insurance – works like an annuity plan). It pays every month for as long as you live, even at 95.
