Back home almost every family has an LIC-type policy: saving and insurance in one, money back at the end. 🇮🇳 In Germany most families protect themselves differently – with pure term life insurance (Risikolebensversicherung), like a term plan.

🇮🇳 Endowment / money-back policy
• Saving + cover mixed in one contract
• You get money back at the end
• Higher premium for that – so the cover is often smaller for the money

🇩🇪 Term life insurance in Germany
• Pure cover, no saving part
• €500,000 cover: net yearly premiums in the latest Finanztest start at about €500 – that's about €42 a month
• If you die during the term, your family gets the money
• Often used to cover a home loan, so your partner can keep the flat

Good to know (Finanztest, 11/2026): 60 tariffs compared, expensive ones can cost more than 3× the cheap ones. Rule of thumb for the sum: 3–5× your yearly salary before tax.
