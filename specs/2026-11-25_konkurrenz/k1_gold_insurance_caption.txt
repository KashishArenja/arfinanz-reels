Many Indian families in Germany keep wedding gold at home. 🇮🇳 But your home insurance (Hausratversicherung) treats gold as "valuables" – and valuables have their own, lower limit.

What many people don't know 👇
🪙 Gold, jewellery, coins and anything made of gold, silver or platinum count as valuables (Wertsachen)
📉 Many tariffs pay valuables only up to about 20% of your total cover – cheap tariffs sometimes only 10%
🧮 Example: €60,000 total cover → about €12,000 for all valuables together
🔐 Not in a proper safe? For gold and jewellery there is often an extra limit of about €20,000
💵 Cash at home is often covered only up to about €1,200–1,500

🇮🇳 Brought gold from India? Make a list, take photos and keep the bills or valuation – after a theft you have to prove what you had.

What you can do: check the valuables limit in your policy, raise it (some tariffs offer 30–50% or more) or keep the gold in a safe or bank locker.
