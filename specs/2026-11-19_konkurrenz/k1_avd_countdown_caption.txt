From 1 January 2027 Germany starts a new pension depot (Altersvorsorgedepot, AVD) – and the state pays a bonus into it every year. 🇩🇪

You pay €150 a month (€1,800 a year):
💶 State bonus: €540 a year (50% on your first €360 + 25% on the rest up to €1,800)
👶 Plus up to €300 a year for each child with Kindergeld (goes to one parent)
📅 The bonus is per year – a year you don't pay in is a bonus you don't get

🇮🇳 In India your employer fills your EPF automatically. In Germany nobody opens this for you – and as an expat you usually have fewer German pension years than your colleagues.

📌 Save this for your January to-do list – and send it to a friend with kids.
