Indians in Germany, listen up 🚨 Save this before your next tax return 📌

Do you send money to your parents in India every month? Then the tax office may give part of it back. It's called support payments (Unterhaltsleistungen, §33a EStG) – and most expats never claim it. 💸

✅ Your parents have little or no income or savings of their own
🏦 You pay by bank transfer – since 2025, cash no longer counts
📄 Your parents sign a support form (Unterhaltserklärung) – the tax office may ask for proof of their income
💶 For India you can deduct up to €3,087 per parent per year (2026: €12,348, India counts ¼)
📅 Pay regularly – a big transfer in December doesn't cover the months before
🧾 Claim it in your tax return with the form "Anlage Unterhalt"

What it can bring: at a 42% tax rate, €3,087 deducted = about €1,297 back per parent – for both parents about €2,593 a year.

Know someone who supports their parents back home? Send this to them 👇

Example calculation, not a guarantee: the amount is reduced if your parents have their own income (above €156 a year for India) or savings, and it must fit your own income. Rules as of Nov 2026 (§33a EStG, BMF country groups: India = group 4). General information, no tax advice – please ask a tax advisor.
