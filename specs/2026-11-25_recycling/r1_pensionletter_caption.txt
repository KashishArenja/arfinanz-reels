Your German pension letter (Renteninformation / Rentenbescheid) shows a big number. But it's the number BEFORE health insurance, care insurance and tax.

Example – €3,500 salary a month before tax, 45 years of work in Germany:
• Letter: about €1,550 a month
• After health + care insurance (about 12%): about €1,360
• After tax (today's rules): about €1,300 in your pocket

And the letter assumes you keep working here until pension age. Came to Germany at 30? Then you have about 37 years, not 45 – the same example shows only about €1,270 on the letter, roughly €1,100 in your pocket.

What you can do now: check your letter, know your real number after deductions, and plan the gap early – with a private pension plan, your employer's pension or a rental flat that is paid off by the time you retire.
