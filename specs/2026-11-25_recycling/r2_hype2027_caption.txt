Everyone is posting "2027 will be the year to buy". Here's the honest version for expats in Germany:

❌ "Prices will explode in 2027"
No. The German Economic Institute (IW Köln) expects about +3% a year until 2035. Right now prices are moving sideways – flats were only +0.3% compared to last year (Q3 2026) and still about 10% below the 2022 peak.

❌ "Cheap loans are back"
No. A 10-year fixed home loan costs about 4% today (best rates around 3.97%, Nov 2026). Nobody can promise cheaper money.

❌ "Banks will give money to everyone"
No. Banks still check your salary, your job, your savings and your visa.

✅ What's real
Since 1 May 2025 German banks have to keep less extra capital aside for home loans (the special risk buffer was cut from 2% to 1%). That gives banks more room to say yes – it doesn't make the loan cheaper.

🇮🇳 On a Blue Card? What matters is your salary, savings and the right bank – not guessing the perfect year.
