🚨 Want to go private in Germany? You need a high salary first:
📅 2026: above €77,400 a year (Versicherungspflichtgrenze)
📅 2027: above €84,150 – approved by the cabinet on 07.10.2026, Bundesrat still to confirm

Before you switch, check:
🩺 Health questions decide your price (and if you're accepted)
👪 Spouse + kids each need their own plan – no "family floater"
📈 Your price depends on your age when you join
🔒 From 55, the way back to public insurance is mostly blocked
