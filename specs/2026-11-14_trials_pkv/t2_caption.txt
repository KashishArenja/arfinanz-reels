Earning €80k in Germany? Your colleagues will tell you: "Go private, it's cheaper!" Sometimes it is. But check these 4 myths first:

❌ "Always cheaper" → ✅ Your price depends on your age and health when you join.
❌ "Family included" → ✅ In private insurance every person has their own plan. No "family floater" like in India.
❌ "I can switch back anytime" → ✅ From age 55 the way back to public insurance is mostly blocked (§6 (3a) SGB V).
❌ "The price stays the same" → ✅ Premiums can rise over the years.

Private can be the right choice – if it fits your life plan (family, staying in Germany or moving back to India).
