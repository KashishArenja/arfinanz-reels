Comment PKV for a free comparison call – we check public vs private for YOUR family 👇

In India, one "family floater" mediclaim covers everyone. In Germany's private health insurance (PKV) there's no family floater: you, your spouse and every child each need their own plan – and each one pays.

🟡 Public (GKV): a spouse without own income and your kids are usually covered for free (family insurance, Familienversicherung). You pay a % of your salary – in 2026 max. about €509 a month for health (employee share, 14.6% + avg. 2.9% extra, salary cap €5,812.50/month).
🔴 Private (PKV): price depends on age and health – per person.

Single, young and healthy? Private can be great. Spouse at home and kids? Do the maths first.

⚠️ If one parent is private and earns more than the other, kids often can't be covered for free in public insurance.

General information, not a guarantee – your price depends on your plan, age and health. Source: Bundesgesundheitsministerium (2026 rates).
