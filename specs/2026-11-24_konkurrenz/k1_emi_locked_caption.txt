Your EMI in India can go up anytime. In Germany 🇩🇪 it's locked for 10 years or more.

🇮🇳 Home loan in India (example SBI)
– Most home loans are floating – the rate is linked to the RBI repo rate (EBLR)
– SBI today: about 7.50–8.70% a year
– When the repo rate goes up, your EMI or your tenure goes up
– Plus: you can prepay a floating loan anytime without a penalty

🇩🇪 Home loan in Germany
– You fix the rate for 10, 15 or even 20+ years (Zinsbindung)
– Best 10-year rate today: 3.97% fixed (4.07% effective)
– Your EMI stays the same every month – you know your number for years
– After 10 years you can repay or switch with 6 months' notice, without a penalty (§489 BGB). Before that, early repayment can cost a fee – many banks allow 5% extra repayment a year.

🇮🇳 Living and earning in euro? Then an EMI that never changes is easier to plan – and German banks do finance Blue Card holders with a permanent job.
