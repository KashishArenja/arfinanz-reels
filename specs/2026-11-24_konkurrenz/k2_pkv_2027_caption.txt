Comment PKV for a free comparison call: private vs public health insurance for your salary 👇

Earn €80,000 in Germany? 🇩🇪 From 2027 that's not enough to switch to private health insurance.

The salary line you must be above (Versicherungspflichtgrenze):
📅 2026: €77,400 a year
📅 2027: €84,150 a year = €7,012.50 a month before tax
📈 That's €6,750 more in one year – it includes an extra one-time increase of €3,600

What it means for you:
✅ Above €84,150 in 2026 AND in 2027? You can leave public insurance at the end of 2026
❌ Between €77,400 and €84,150? You stay public in 2027
🔒 Already private before 2027? A lower line of €80,550 applies to you – check where you stand

🇮🇳 Starting a new job in Germany in 2027 (Blue Card, transfer)? With €80,000 you start in public insurance – plan your health insurance before you sign.

Private is not automatically better: costs rise with age and every family member needs their own contract. That's why we compare both for your situation.
