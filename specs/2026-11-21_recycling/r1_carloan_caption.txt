Nobody in Germany stays a renter because of a €5 coffee. ☕
But a car on EMI? That can push your own home years away.

Why: German banks look at every monthly payment you already have. Each one lowers what you can still pay for a home loan.

Example: €600 a month for a car
➡️ Home loan at 4.01% interest + 2% repayment = about 6% of the loan per year
➡️ €600 × 12 ÷ 6% ≈ €120,000 less home loan

That's not a coffee – that's a kitchen, a balcony, a whole extra room. 🏠

🇮🇳 Back home a car EMI feels normal. In Germany it can cost you the flat. Buying in the next 1–2 years? Skip the new car EMI first.
