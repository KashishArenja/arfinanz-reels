3 myths many expats in Germany believe about losing their income:

❌ "The state pays my salary." → The full state disability pension (Erwerbsminderungsrente) was on average €1,117 a month at the end of 2025 – and only if you can't work 3 hours a day in ANY job, not just your own.
❌ "It only happens after accidents." → The #1 cause of occupational disability in Germany is mental health (about 36% of cases, e.g. burnout, depression). Accidents: about 7%.
❌ "My employer keeps paying." → Your employer pays 6 weeks. Then sick pay (Krankengeld), max 78 weeks.

Disability insurance (Berufsunfähigkeitsversicherung, BU) pays a fixed monthly amount if you can't do YOUR job anymore. The younger and healthier you are when you sign, the easier it is.

Causes: Morgen & Morgen via GDV (08.05.2025).
Example calculation, not a guarantee – BU benefits depend on your contract, age, health and job. State figure: average full disability pension (volle Erwerbsminderungsrente) end of 2025, Bundestag hib 909/2026.
