5 things expats in Germany often learn too late about burnout and money:

💼 Your employer pays your salary for 6 weeks.
🏥 Then sick pay from health insurance (Krankengeld) – max 78 weeks.
🏛️ The state disability pension only comes if you can't work 3 hours a day in ANY job.
📉 The average full disability pension: €1,117 a month (end of 2025).
🧠 The #1 cause of occupational disability is mental health – about 36% of cases.

Disability insurance (Berufsunfähigkeitsversicherung, BU) pays a monthly amount if you can't do YOUR job anymore. Save this list.
