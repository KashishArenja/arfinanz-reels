Burnout, back problems or a long illness – what happens to your money in Germany?

💼 Week 1–6: your employer pays your full salary
🏥 Week 7–78: sick pay (Krankengeld) from your health insurance – about 70% of your gross salary, max. 90% of your net
🏛️ After that: the state disability pension – on average €1,117 a month (full pension, end of 2025), and only if you can't work 3 hours a day in ANY job

Rent, EMI and money for your family in India don't stop. 🇮🇳

✅ Disability insurance (Berufsunfähigkeitsversicherung, BU) pays a monthly amount you choose if you can't do YOUR job anymore.
