If burnout, back problems or an illness stop you from working in Germany, your salary doesn't just continue.

Your employer pays 6 weeks. Then health insurance sick pay (Krankengeld) for max 78 weeks. After that, the state disability pension (Erwerbsminderungsrente) – on average €1,117 a month for the full pension, and only if you can't work 3 hours a day in ANY job.

On €3,500 net that's a gap of €2,383 every month – rent, EMI and family support in India still need to be paid.

Disability insurance (Berufsunfähigkeitsversicherung, BU) closes this gap: it pays a monthly amount you choose if you can't do YOUR job anymore. Save this table and check your own number.
