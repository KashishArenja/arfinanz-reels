You found your dream home in Germany – with a garden. ☀️ But nobody tells you: in winter the sun sits very low, and houses and trees next door can cover your garden in shadow for months.

Check it in 2 minutes with the free app ShadeMap (shademap.app):
1️⃣ Type in the address
2️⃣ Move the time slider – watch the shadows of houses & trees live
3️⃣ Set the date to 21 December – the day with the longest shadows of the year
4️⃣ Check your balcony at 6 pm – will you get evening sun in summer?
5️⃣ At the viewing: a sun-path app on your phone camera shows where the sun goes

South or west facing = more sun, and often easier to sell later. 💰
