The dream is the same – a home for your family. 🏠 The system in Germany is just different.

Buying in 3–8 years, not tomorrow? Many Germans use a building savings contract (Bausparvertrag) for exactly this:

📌 You save a fixed amount every month towards your own capital
📌 The interest rate for your later loan is fixed in the contract from day 1 – no matter where rates go
📌 State bonus possible: 10% on up to €700 a year (€1,400 for married couples) = up to €70 / €140 a year (Wohnungsbauprämie – taxable income up to €35,000 / €70,000, money must go into a home)
📌 Watch the costs: usually a fee of about 1% of the contract sum and low interest on your savings – so check if it fits your plan

🇮🇳 New in Germany and no long credit history yet? Starting to save early shows the bank you can handle a monthly payment.
