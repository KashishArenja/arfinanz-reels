Thinking about moving back to India one day? 🇮🇳 Every year you work in Germany keeps paying you – for life, even in India.

Find your years (example: €60,000 gross a year):
5 years → about €246 a month
8 years → about €393 a month
10 years → about €491 a month
15 years → about €737 a month
20 years → about €982 a month
25 years → about €1,228 a month

✅ Germany and India have a social security agreement – your passport doesn't matter.
✅ The German pension insurance (Deutsche Rentenversicherung) pays to your bank account in India.
✅ Less than 5 years in Germany? Your insurance years in India can help you reach the minimum.
⚠️ The amount only comes from your German years – that's why your German colleague, who started here at 22, gets more.

👉 Before you leave: request your pension record (Versicherungsverlauf) and check that every German year is in it.
