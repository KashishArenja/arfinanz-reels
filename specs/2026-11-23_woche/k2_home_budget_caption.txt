How much home can your salary buy in Germany? 🇩🇪 Find your net salary on slide 3.

Home loans today: about 4.5% fixed for 10 years, best offers around 4.1%. Rule of thumb: EMI ≈ 35% of your net salary.
💶 €3,000 net → €1,050 EMI → about €194k loan
💶 €4,000 net → €1,400 EMI → about €258k loan
💶 €5,000 net → €1,750 EMI → about €323k loan

On top come the buying costs, about 5.5–12% of the price (property transfer tax 3.5–6.5% by state, notary + land registry ~2%, agent up to 3.57%) – most banks want these from your savings.
