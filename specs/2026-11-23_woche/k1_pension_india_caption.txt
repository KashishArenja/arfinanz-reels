Moving back to India one day? Your German pension doesn't stay behind. 🇮🇳🇩🇪 Thanks to the India–Germany social security agreement, it can be paid to your bank account in India.

Find your years (slide 4):
💶 10 years at €75,000 a year ≈ €614 a month
💶 20 years at €75,000 a year ≈ €1,228 a month
💶 Max. about €83 a month for every year you earn €101,400 or more

The rules:
✅ At least 5 years of contributions in Germany
📅 Paid from retirement age (67 for most of us) – not when you leave
🌍 You can apply from India

Your German colleague works here 40+ years. You maybe 10–15. That's the gap to plan for – while you still earn in euros.
