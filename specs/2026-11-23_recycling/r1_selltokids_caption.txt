Most parents in Germany simply gift their flat to their kids. Smart families sell it instead – completely legal. 🇩🇪

Why selling can beat gifting:
🏠 Parents ↔ children = no real estate transfer tax (Grunderwerbsteuer, §3 No. 6 GrEStG – direct family line)
💶 You give your kids a loan for the price (vendor loan, Verkäuferdarlehen). They pay you back every month – the rent from the flat helps them pay.
📉 Because your kids BUY the flat, they can depreciate the building again (AfA) – that can lower their tax if the flat is rented out.

⚠️ The part nobody mentions: it only works with a realistic market price and a real loan contract (real interest, real repayments, everything in writing). Otherwise the tax office can treat it as a hidden gift.
