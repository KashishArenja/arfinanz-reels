Most expats in Germany know two ways to deal with taxes: pay them or complain about them. 😅 There's a third one – in German tax law since 2005.

The Rürup pension (Basisrente) – you can fill it with ETFs: what you pay in is 100% tax-deductible in 2026. Example, you pay in €5,000 a year:
💶 €50,000 taxable income → €1,713 back
💶 €60,000 → €1,886 back
💶 €80,000 → €2,100 back (42%)
💶 €300,000 → €2,250 back (45%)

🇮🇳 Think of it like 80C back home – just much bigger: up to €30,826 a year in 2026 (€61,652 for married couples).

⚠️ Know the catch before you sign:
• Employees: your statutory pension contributions (yours + employer's) count towards the €30,826 – your room is smaller.
• The money is locked: no lump sum, no early access – paid out only as a lifelong monthly pension (from 62 at the earliest).
• Planning to move back to India? Check how the payout will be taxed before you start.
