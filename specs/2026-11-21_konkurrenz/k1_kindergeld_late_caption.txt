Kids just moved from India to Germany? Save this checklist before you lose a single month of Kindergeld 📌

In Germany you get €259 a month per child (Kindergeld 2026) – that's €3,108 a year per child. But many new expats apply months later, because nobody tells them. 💸

🪪 Blue Card or a residence permit that lets you work usually counts
👧 Your kids live with you in Germany (registered at your address)
🔢 Each child needs a German tax ID (Steuer-ID) – it comes by post after the Anmeldung
💻 Apply online at the Familienkasse of the Federal Employment Agency
📅 Only the last 6 months before your application are paid back
🇮🇳 Kids still living in India = no Kindergeld from Germany

Example: kids arrived in January, you apply in the next January → only 6 of 12 months are paid. That's €1,554 lost per child.
