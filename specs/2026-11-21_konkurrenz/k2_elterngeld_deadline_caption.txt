Baby born in Germany? Save this before the first 3 months are over 📌

Germany pays parents Elterngeld (parental allowance) while they stay home with the baby: about 65% of your net pay, between €300 and €1,800 a month. Earn €3,000 net or more? Then you usually get the full €1,800. 👶

🪪 Blue Card or a residence permit that lets you work usually counts
💶 About 65% of your net pay before the birth, max. €1,800 a month
👫 Both parents take at least 2 months = up to 14 months for the family
📅 Only the last 3 months before your application are paid back
📄 You need the birth certificate first – then apply at your state's Elterngeld office
🇮🇳 No grandparents here to remind you – put it in your calendar now

Example: you apply 6 months after the birth → the first 3 months are gone. At €1,800 that's €5,400 lost.

Good to know: maternity pay (Mutterschaftsgeld) in the first weeks is counted against Elterngeld, and a household income above €175,000 taxable a year gets nothing.
