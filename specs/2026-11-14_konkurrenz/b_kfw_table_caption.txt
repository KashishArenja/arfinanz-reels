Buying an old house or flat with kids? The KfW loan "Jung kauft Alt" (KfW = Germany's state-owned development bank, programme 308) gives families a low-interest loan – if the home needs energy renovation.

How much you can get 👇
👶 1 child: income max €90,000 → loan up to €140,000
👶👶 2 kids: income max €100,000 → up to €160,000
👶👶👶 3 kids: income max €110,000 → up to €180,000
➕ Every extra child: +€10,000 income limit (loan stays max €180,000)

"Income" = taxable household income, average of the 2nd and 3rd year before you apply (your German tax assessments, "Steuerbescheide"). The loan can't be higher than the purchase price.

🇮🇳 Own a flat in India? Still OK – only homes in Germany count. Nobody in your household may own a home in Germany.
✅ The home must have energy class F, G or H, you live in it yourself and renovate within 4.5 years.
📝 Apply BEFORE you buy.

Source: KfW leaflet 308, as of 08/2026. Funding rules can change, approval depends on KfW and your bank. Arenja & Raj Finanz GmbH – Immobiliardarlehensvermittler §34i GewO, Versicherungsmakler §34d GewO. No tax or legal advice.
