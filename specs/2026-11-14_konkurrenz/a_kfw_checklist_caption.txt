Own a flat in India? Many expats think that rules them out. For the KfW family loan "Jung kauft Alt" (KfW = Germany's state-owned development bank, programme 308), only homes in Germany count. 🇮🇳🇩🇪

✅ Check yourself:
👶 At least 1 child under 18 lives with you
🏚️ You buy an existing house or flat in Germany with energy class F, G or H – and live in it yourself
🔑 Nobody in your household owns a home in Germany
💶 Taxable household income max €90,000 with 1 child, +€10,000 per extra child (average of the 2nd and 3rd year before you apply – you need your German tax assessments, "Steuerbescheide")
🔧 You renovate within 4.5 years (to Efficiency House 85 EE or the required single measures)
📝 Apply through your bank BEFORE you buy – or sign the purchase contract with a condition

💶 Loan amount: up to €140,000 (1 child), €160,000 (2 kids), €180,000 (3+ kids) – at a low KfW interest rate.

📌 Save this before your next house viewing.
