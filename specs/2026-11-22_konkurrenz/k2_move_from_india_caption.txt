Moved from India to Germany for your job? Save this checklist 📌 – your move can lower your German tax.

A move for work counts as work costs (Werbungskosten, §9 EStG) – a move from India to Germany too. 🇮🇳✈️🇩🇪 Many expats never claim it.

✈️ Flights for you, your partner and your kids
📦 Shipping and transport of your household things
🏠 Agent fee for your rented flat in Germany
❌ Anything your employer paid or paid back does NOT count
🧾 Claim it in your tax return for the year you moved (Anlage N, work costs) – keep tickets and invoices
📅 Moved in 2022? The voluntary return for 2022 ends on 31 December 2026

Example (move in 2025, 30% tax rate): flights €2,400 + shipping €1,600 = €4,000 work costs. Everyone gets €1,230 work costs automatically – the €2,770 above that brings about €831 back.

Good to know: your Indian salary from the months before the move can raise your German tax rate in the arrival year (progression clause, §32b EStG). Worth having someone check that first return.
