Never filed a tax return in Germany? Save this checklist 📌 – for 2022 your last day is 31 December 2026.

As an employee in tax class 1 you often don't HAVE to file. So many expats never do – and leave their refund with the tax office. 💸

💶 Average refund in Germany: €1,172 (Destatis, tax year 2021)
📅 Voluntary return for 2022: last day 31.12.2026 – this deadline can't be extended
🔁 2023, 2024 and 2025 you can still file later – up to 4 years back
✈️ What often counts: your move from India for the job, work laptop and phone, your way to work (2022: €0.30 per km, from km 21 €0.38)
🧾 File online for free with ELSTER – or with a tax app or tax advisor
🇮🇳 Interest from your Indian FD or savings account has to be declared in Germany too

Obliged to file (e.g. tax class 3/5 or extra income)? Then other, earlier deadlines apply – this is only for voluntary returns.
