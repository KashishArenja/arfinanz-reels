Same €350,000 home loan. Same 4% interest. The only difference: how fast you pay it back (Tilgung). 🏠

🐢 1% repayment → €1,461 EMI a month → loan gone in 40 years → €355,800 interest
🚶 2% repayment → €1,753 EMI → 27.5 years → €228,100 interest
🏃 3% repayment → €2,045 EMI → 21 years → €170,200 interest

€584 more per month saves you about €185,000 in interest – and almost 19 years of payments. 🤯

Many buyers in Germany simply take the lowest EMI. That's good for the bank, not for you. You choose your repayment rate when you sign.

🇮🇳 In India you can usually prepay a floating-rate home loan any time. In Germany extra payments (Sondertilgung) during the fixed-rate period are only possible if your contract says so – agree on them before you sign.

The right EMI depends on your income, your plans and your family – there's no one-size-fits-all.
