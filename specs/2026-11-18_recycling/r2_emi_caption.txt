A lower monthly EMI sounds good. But on a €350,000 home loan you pay the bank about €185,000 more in total. 🏠

Home loan €350,000, 4% interest:
€1,461 a month → you pay for 40 years → €355,800 extra to the bank
€1,607 → 33 years → €276,800
€1,753 → 27 years → €228,100
€1,899 → 24 years → €194,700
€2,045 → 21 years → €170,200
€2,336 → 17 years → €136,300

€584 more EMI a month (€2,045 instead of €1,461) = €185,000 less interest and 19 years less paying. 🤯

In Germany YOU choose your EMI when you sign (the "Tilgung"). Many people take the smallest one – that's good for the bank, not for you.

🇩🇪 Choose your EMI before you sign. In Germany paying extra later (Sondertilgung) only works if it's written in your contract – in India you can usually pay extra any time.
