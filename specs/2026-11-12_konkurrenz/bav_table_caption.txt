Moved to Germany later in life? Then this table matters for you. 🇩🇪

With a company pension (bAV), part of your gross salary goes into a pension plan. If your employer saves social security that way, they must add 15% on top (§1a BetrAVG).

📊 What your employer adds in 30 years (e.g. from 37 to 67):
€100/month → +€15/month → +€5,400
€200/month → +€30/month → +€10,800
€338/month (the 2026 maximum, 4% of €8,450) → +€50.70/month → +€18,252

That's before any returns – just the money your employer puts in.

👉 Ask HR: "Do we offer Entgeltumwandlung with the employer subsidy (Arbeitgeberzuschuss) under §1a BetrAVG?"

📌 Save this before your next talk with HR.
